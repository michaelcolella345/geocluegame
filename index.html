GeoClue

Find today's mystery country. Each wrong guess unlocks an easier clue and shows how far off you are, plus which way to head.

Clues

        Hot: under 500 mi Warm: 500–1,500 Mild: 1,500–3,500 Cold: 3,500+
        Distances are measured between country centers. New puzzle every day at midnight.

        How to play

        Guess the mystery country in 5 tries. Everyone gets the same country each day.

        • You start with one clue, the hardest. Each wrong guess unlocks an easier one.
        • After a wrong guess you'll see how far away it is (center to center), an arrow pointing toward the answer, and a color: Hot under 500 mi, Warm under 1,500, Mild under 3,500, Cold farther.
        • Common names work too: USA, UK, Ivory Coast, Czech Republic…

        A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight, your time.
