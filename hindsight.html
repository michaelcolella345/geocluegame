Hindsight is a daily game from GeoClue. New matchups every day at midnight.

How to play

Each day brings six matchups of two events from history. Everyone gets the same ones.

  • Tap the event you think happened first. Then you'll see both years on a timeline.
  • Each right pick is worth 1 point. Get 2 in a row and every right pick after that is worth 2 🔥, until you miss one. A perfect day is 10 points.
  • The matchups jump all over history, from ancient Egypt to last decade.

At the end you'll see how you did against everyone who played today. Come back tomorrow for a new set.
